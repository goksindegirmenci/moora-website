Stays with you

  • Your product and production
  • Strategic buyer relationships
  • Brand direction and growth decisions
  • Visibility into every order, stock level and payment

Moora runs

  • Order intake across website, email and WhatsApp

Getting started

Start small and move in stages

No big-bang switch. You agree each stage before the next begins.

Stage one

Stock and direct-to-consumer

Your inventory arrives at the Moora warehouse and online orders start flowing through the platform.

Stage two

Buyers in agreed batches

Wholesale buyers move across in groups you choose, so nothing is dropped.

Stage three

Optimise with your data

With orders and stock in one place, reporting and forecasting start working for you.
